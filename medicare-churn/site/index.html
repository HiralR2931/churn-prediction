A decade-long migration, and a sudden slowdown

Each fall, people on Medicare choose their coverage for the next year, and those choices take effect on January 1. Comparing a county's Medicare Advantage (MA) share in December with the following February therefore shows how many people moved from Original Medicare to a private MA plan that season.

05

Can we predict which counties lead?

Four ways of ranking counties were tested in a walk-forward backtest: for each season from 2019 on, every method sees only earlier years, then ranks counties by expected switching. A score of 1.0 would mean a perfect ranking.

06

2026 outlook: Dec 2025 → Feb 2026

Each county is scored with the blend, which was the most reliable method in the backtest. Values are relative to the median county, whatever its pace turns out to be. A county at +1.0 pp is expected to switch one point faster than the median county. CMS has not yet published February 2026, so these forecasts are unscored.

08

Method and caveats

Data. CMS Medicare Monthly Enrollment, county level, January 2013 – December 2025 (≈564k rows). Counts of 1–10 are suppressed by CMS and imputed at 5.

Switching. Change in MA share between December and the following February. This is a net measure: it also moves when people join or leave Medicare, and when plans close mid-year. CMS does not publish individual transitions. "MA" is CMS's "Medicare Advantage and other health plans" count, which includes a small number of cost plans and PACE.

National vs. county figures. National shares and totals use CMS's national rows. About 0.5 million people have no county on file, so county figures sum to slightly less. County comparisons are made against the median county each season, not the national total.

Small counties. Counties with fewer than 1,000 beneficiaries move several points on a handful of people. They are greyed out on the map and left out of the statistics and models.

Forecasting. Rankings are scored on seasons each method never saw. The blend weights the county's own last season and its state's median last season equally. Correlations describe associations, not causes.

Connecticut. Connecticut replaced its 8 counties with 9 planning regions in 2022. Its Dec 2021 → Feb 2022 season cannot be measured, its first seasons as regions have no prior-season history for forecasting, and the county boundaries used for the map predate the change, so it is not drawn.
